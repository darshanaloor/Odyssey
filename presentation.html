Siloed Bookings

Travellers book across 4-5 disconnected apps (RedBus, MakeMyTrip, Uber, etc.). No platform knows about the traveller's complete multi-leg journey.

The Domino Effect

A disruption in Leg 1 silently ruins Legs 2 and 3 with zero cross-operator awareness. Airlines and hotels treat it as an unexcused no-show.

Refund Blindspots

Opaque cancellation deductions, delayed chargebacks (5-7 business days), manual disputes, and no single auditable refund ledger.

Traditional Travel Booking

  • ✖ Passive Ticket Vending: Apps sell independent tickets and abandon the traveller post-sale.
  • ✖ Fragmented Operator Silos: Bus company doesn't care if train leaves before arrival.
  • ✖ Midnight Scramble: Travellers call hotlines or re-purchase tickets out-of-pocket.
  • ✖ Opaque Deductions: Missed hotel check-ins are 100% forfeited with zero recourse.
